WESTSIDE TEST - HUONG DAN CHO QUAN NET (tu ban 1000094)
=========================================================

Tu ban 1000094, may co phan mem quan ly phong may (CCBoot, iCafe8, gCafe,
CyberStation, CSM...) KHONG con bi launcher dung lai truoc Easy Anti-Cheat.
May quan net mo game qua EAC giong may ca nhan.

1. Cai san dich vu EAC vao image (khuyen nghi cho may diskless/dong bang)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
EAC can dich vu Windows "EasyAntiCheat_EOS" (cai bang quyen Administrator).
May diskless hoac co phan mem dong bang se mat dich vu nay sau moi lan khoi
dong lai, nen lan mo game nao cung phai cai lai va hien hop UAC. Cach tranh:

  a. Vao che do ghi image (Super/Admin workstation cua phan mem quan ly).
  b. Mo Command Prompt bang "Run as administrator", chuyen den thu muc:
       <thu muc WESTSIDE TEST>\WESTSIDE CITY.app\EasyAntiCheat
  c. Chay bo cai chinh thuc cua Easy Anti-Cheat:
       EasyAntiCheat_EOS_Setup.exe install bc82900358664953a3be2ceea8697916
     Neu dich vu da co nhung bi loi:
       EasyAntiCheat_EOS_Setup.exe repair bc82900358664953a3be2ceea8697916
  d. Mo WESTSIDE TEST mot lan, vao server cho EAC chay xong, thoat game.
  e. Luu (commit) image theo cach cua phan mem quan ly.

Ma tren la Product ID cong khai cua WESTSIDE TEST, khong phai mat khau.
Khi WESTSIDE TEST cap nhat EAC, lam lai buoc c-e neu launcher bao loi EAC.

2. Nhung viec KHONG lam
-----------------------
- Khong tat Windows Defender/antivirus, Secure Boot hay kiem tra chu ky driver.
- Khong xoa/sua file trong WESTSIDE CITY.app de "ne" EAC.
- Neu phan mem quan ly co danh sach driver duoc phep, hay cho phep driver/dich
  vu EasyAntiCheat_EOS thay vi tat bao ve.

3. Khi may bi man hinh xanh hoac EAC khong chay
-----------------------------------------------
- Neu Windows bi man hinh xanh sau khi mo game, lan mo sau launcher se tu dung
  an toan. Chup ma loi tren man hinh xanh, lay file trong C:\Windows\Minidump
  (neu co) va file WESTSIDE CITY.app\westside-eac-bootstrap.log gui admin.
- Admin co the duyet IP public cua quan (co thoi han) neu EAC van khong chay
  duoc tren he thong cua quan; may duoc duyet chay che do tuong thich.

4. May ao (VMware, VirtualBox, Hyper-V, QEMU/KVM...)
----------------------------------------------------
Easy Anti-Cheat co chinh sach rieng ve may ao; launcher khong the va khong
duoc bo qua chinh sach do. Nguoi choi can may that, hoac lien he admin.
